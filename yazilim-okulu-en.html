Your typical day with us:

8:30 AM

Good morning!

Your first session starts in 30 minutes. Grab breakfast and get ready for a full day working toward your new career.

9–10 AM

Pair-programming session with a teaching assistant

Every morning, you'll pair up with fellow students — seeing how others approach problems, picking up new techniques on the spot, and practicing how to solve problems as a team.

10 AM–12 PM

Discussion session with the head instructor

You'll go deep on the day's topic, ask questions directly, and explore the subject from multiple angles with our most experienced instructors.

12–5 PM

Self-study & study jam

Once live sessions wrap up, you'll keep practicing with your peers and teaching assistants — reviewing recordings, working through exercises, and preparing for the next day.

Financing

Make it work for your budget

Cost shouldn't be what stands between you and a new career.

Bildungsgutschein

Germany · Free with a training voucher

If you're unemployed, at risk of becoming unemployed, or a recognized refugee residing in Germany, you may be eligible for a Bildungsgutschein (education voucher) issued by the Bundesagentur für Arbeit or your local Jobcenter — which can cover your entire tuition. If this applies to you, reach out and we'll help you understand the process.

Installment plan

Everywhere

Pay an initial deposit, then split the rest of your tuition into interest-free installments over up to 6 months.
